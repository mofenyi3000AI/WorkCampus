# Work@Campus: Office Admin Guide

This guide is for the Work@Campus office admin. It shows where to click, step by step. Words in **bold** are the exact words on the button or the screen.

App link: **https://workcampus.onrender.com**

---

## Part 1: Set up (one time, 10 minutes)

1. On your laptop, open **Google Chrome** and go to **https://workcampus.onrender.com**
   - The first time, it can take up to 1 minute to open. That is normal. Wait.
2. Tap **Log in or join**.
3. Scroll to **Join free**. Fill in your details and tap **Create free account**.
4. Send your email address to the owner on WhatsApp. Say: "I've joined. Please make me admin."
5. When the owner says it's done: tap **Me** (bottom right), tap **Log out**, then log in again.
6. Tap **Me** again. You now see a button called **Admin desk**. That is your work screen.
7. Save the app in Chrome: tap the **star icon** at the right end of the address bar to bookmark it.

**Never share your password.** If you forget it, ask the owner.

---

## Part 2: Your daily routine (15 to 30 minutes a day)

Open the app → **Me** → **Admin desk**. Work from top to bottom.

### Step 1: Students to verify
New students send a photo of their **student card** and a **selfie**. You check that they are real students.

For each student, check all 4 things:
1. The student card is clear and you can read it.
2. The **name and surname** on the card match the name written above the photos.
3. The face on the card looks like the person in the selfie.
4. The card is from the right university.

- All 4 correct → tap **Approve**.
- Anything wrong, or the photo is blurry → tap **Reject**. The student will be asked to send new photos.

The photos are **deleted automatically** after you tap Approve or Reject.
**Never** screenshot, save, download or forward these photos. They are private ID documents and the law (POPIA) protects them.

### Step 2: Market items to approve
Students and businesses sell items. Each item waits for you before anyone can see it.

- **Approve** if: it is a real item (books, calculators, kettles, fridges, clothes, furniture) with a sensible price.
- **Remove** if: alcohol, drugs, weapons, medicine, stolen-looking goods, finished assignments or exam answers, anything rude, or anything that looks like a scam.
- Not sure? Leave it and ask the owner.

### Step 3: Food requests
Students who need food ask here quietly. **This is the most private part of the app.**

1. Read the request. You see the student's name, student number, email and what they need.
2. Send the request details to the owner on WhatsApp (name and what they need only).
3. When the student has been helped, tap **Mark helped**.

**Never** tell anyone who asked for food. Not friends, not family, not in a group. Not even as a joke.

### Step 4: Check new posts
1. Tap **Gigs** at the bottom. Read the newest posts. Do the same on **Walk**.
2. If a post is rude, fake, a scam, asks someone to write an assignment or exam for them, or is not safe: tap the post → scroll down → tap **Remove post**.
3. If you are not sure, take a screenshot and ask the owner.

### Step 5: Forgot password
A member messages you that they forgot their password:
1. On the Admin desk, go to **Forgot password**.
2. Type **their** email address. Tap **Make temporary password**.
3. Send the temporary password to that member privately (WhatsApp or email).
4. Tell them: "Log in with this, then go to **Me → Change my password**."

Only do this if the message comes from that person (their own number or email). If in doubt, ask the owner.

### Step 6: Payments
Under **Payments** you see every payment that went through.
If a member says "I paid but my plan is not on": find their payment here.
- If it is there: tell the owner the member's name and the time.
- If it is not there: ask the member for a screenshot of their PayFast receipt and send it to the owner.

**Never** take money from members yourself, and never give out bank details. All payments go through the app.

## Part 3: Getting new members (marketing)

On the Admin desk, the card **Links to share** shows the link for each university, for example **https://workcampus.onrender.com/ul**. When you post this link in WhatsApp or Facebook, it shows a picture.

Your posting routine:
1. The owner gives you the messages to post.
2. Post in each WhatsApp or Facebook group **once every 3 or 4 days**, not more. Posting too often gets you removed from groups.
3. Always follow the group's rules. If a group says "no adverts", don't post there.
4. When someone asks a question in the group, answer kindly and send the link.
5. When someone posts "I'm looking for a room / a bakkie / someone to help me move / a study partner", reply with: "Post it on Work@Campus, students there can help: https://workcampus.onrender.com/ul"

---

## Part 4: Things only the owner does
You will not see these on your Admin desk. That is normal:
- Money totals, promo codes, adding admins or ambassadors, adding universities, paying ambassadors.

---

## Part 5: Problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app takes long to open | Wait 1 minute. It was sleeping. Then it is fast. |
| "Please log in again" | Log in again with your email and password. |
| I can't see **Admin desk** under Me | Log out and log in again. Still missing? Tell the owner. |
| A student is rude or threatening | Screenshot it. Send it to the owner. Don't argue. |
| Someone says they are in danger | Tell them to call **10111** (SAPS) or campus security right away. Then tell the owner. |
| Anything else | Screenshot the screen and send it to the owner on WhatsApp. |

---

## The 5 golden rules
1. Private information stays private: ID photos, food requests, names and student numbers.
2. Never share your password, and never use someone else's account.
3. Never take money or give out bank details.
4. Be kind and polite to every member.
5. When in doubt, ask the owner first.
